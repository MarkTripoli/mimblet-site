Read together

Twenty original, eight-page stories with short pages made for your voice. Explore a garden, visit a rabbit's cottage, or follow a pocket moon. Choose larger text or a softer night-reading palette.

38 picture cards

Look slowly

Six CC0 photographs, 24 clearly disclosed AI-generated photo-style images, and eight original patterns. Black-and-white, red, yellow, and mixed red-and-yellow palettes, at your own pace.

The AI-generated photo-style cards are generated images, not camera photographs. No flashing or animated slideshows.

4 original rhymes

A little finger play

Simple verses and gentle suggestions for your hands. Your face and voice are the most interesting part; enjoy these moments without showing the screen, too.

3 original sound loops

A quiet moment

Soft Rain, Slow Waves, and Gentle Lullaby. Sound starts only when you press Play. Set a 5-, 10-, or 15-minute stop timer; playback pauses when the app becomes inactive and never resumes on its own.

Less setup. More together.

A library, not a tracking app.

Stories, cards, rhymes, fonts, and sounds come with the app. Once installed, Mimblet needs no connection, account, subscription, or runtime content download. New content arrives only through a new app-store version.

Only the favorites and preferences you choose are saved locally. No child profiles, reading history, analytics, cloud sync, or in-app purchases.

A gentle cue for grown-ups

Mimblet is for short, shared moments, operated by an adult. Put the device away when your infant turns away or needs a break. Keep sounds quiet and the device out of your baby's reach and sleep space. Mimblet is not a medical device, developmental assessment, or sleep treatment.
